I'm Javier Carvajal — an **Application Security Engineer** and Red Team
enthusiast with 2+ years of experience integrating security into the Software
Development Life Cycle and hardening corporate infrastructure.

My day job is vulnerability assessment, threat emulation, and Zero-Trust
architecture: aligning offensive testing (OWASP Top 10, MITRE ATT&CK) with risk
mitigation that actually protects the asset (CVSS). This site is the other half
of that — the lab work, written down.

## Why this blog exists

Writing an exploit chain down forces me to understand it. A shell I can't
explain isn't knowledge, it's luck. So I document every machine end to end,
including the paths that went nowhere — those are usually the instructive part.

## What you'll find here

- **CTF write-ups** — machines from VulnHub and HackTheBox, from enumeration
  through privilege escalation, with full command output.
- **eJPT study notes** — structured notes as I work toward the eLearnSecurity
  Junior Penetration Tester certification.
- **Tooling notes** — Nmap, Burp Suite, Metasploit, Wazuh, and the Linux side of
  post-exploitation.
